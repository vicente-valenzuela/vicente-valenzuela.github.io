PUBLICATIONS (see also Google Scholar)

I am a plasma physicist with broad interests in laboratory astrophysics and astrophysical theory. My research develops and uses pulsed-power and laser-driven platforms, supported by laser, self-emission, and particle diagnostics, together with analytic theory and numerical simulations, to study fundamental processes relevant to astrophysical plasmas.
Since moving to Livermore I have become interested about fundamental processes relevant to inertial confinement fusion, most prominently cross-beam energy transfer and burn.
