I am a plasma physicist with broad interests in laboratory astrophysics and astrophysical theory. My research develops and uses pulsed-power and laser-driven platforms, supported by plasma diagnostics, analytic theory, and numerical simulations, to study fundamental processes in astrophysical plasmas.

My work encompasses angular momentum transport, particle acceleration and heating in collisionless shocks and magnetic reconnection, turbulence, and the structure and evolution of collimated plasma jets.

Email Google Scholar GitHub CV

Research

  • Rotating plasmas and accretion physics. Laboratory platforms for angular momentum transport, quasi-Keplerian flows, and magnetized rotating winds.
  • Collisionless shocks and magnetic reconnection. Experiments and modeling of particle heating, acceleration, and energy partition in high-energy-density plasmas.
  • Diagnostics and inference. Thomson scattering, x-ray imaging, spectroscopy, and proton radiography for laboratory astrophysics experiments.
