I am a plasma physicist with broad interests in laboratory astrophysics and astrophysical theory. My research develops and uses pulsed-power and laser-driven platforms, supported by laser, self-emission, and particle diagnostics, together with analytic theory and numerical simulations, to study fundamental processes relevant to astrophysical plasmas. You can find a list of my publications and a summary of my research* below.
*...work in progress...

Since moving to Livermore, I have become interested in fundamental processes relevant to inertial confinement fusion, particularly cross-beam energy transfer and thermonuclear burn.

Short bio
Vicente is a Lawrence Postdoctoral Fellow at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and Visting Scientist at MIT where he investigates magnetized rotating plasma flows. He graduated with a PhD in Plasma Physics from Imperial College, after which he moved to Princeton University as a postdoc in Astrophysical Sciences where he studied particle heating in magnetic reconnection and collisionless shocks.
